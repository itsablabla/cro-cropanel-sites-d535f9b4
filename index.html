Where the Conversion
Leaks Are

The single most valuable conversion mechanism on this site is the coverage-check form, which is the primary qualification step for every plan. The funnel is bleeding value because the homepage and plans pages present multiple competing CTAs and duplicate forms, diluting user focus and creating ambiguity about the primary conversion path, while the page's heavy script load and slow LCP (48.9s) cause most visitors to bounce before they even reach the form.
